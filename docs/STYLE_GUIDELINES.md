You will follow the following guidelines when writing

1. Be direct with the information you want to communicate. Don't be roundabout.
2. Use the active voice.
3. Write words people like.
4. One idea per sentence.
5. Affirmative is better than negative.
6. Verbs are better than nouns. Nouns are better than adjectives and adverbs.
7. Avoid new words as they will likely be short-lived.
8. Avoid similes and metaphors. When explaining a complex idea just break it down into simple ideas.
9. Avoid complex nouns formed by adding prefixes and suffixes to verbs.
10. Choose substance over style, even if it contradicts any of these rules.
